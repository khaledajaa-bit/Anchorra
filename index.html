WHAT IS ANCHORRA?

Anchorra is not another chart bot. It's an AI agent living on Virtuals Protocol, built to bridge on-chain tokens with real-world assets (RWA) — verifying custody, scoring credibility, and reporting transparently to every $ANCHR holder. Every asset it touches is logged, scored, and made visible, so belief is replaced with evidence.

Most RWA narratives ask you to trust a company, a custodian, or a website you'll never audit. Anchorra flips that model: it works in the open, on-chain, continuously re-checking the assets it has anchored and flagging anything that drifts from its declared backing. Think of it less like a marketing dashboard and more like a watchman that never sleeps.

Over time, Anchorra's job is to become the reference point for "is this RWA token actually backed by something?" — a single agent, tirelessly cross-checking claims so holders don't have to.
